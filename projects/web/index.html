Capabilities

Built for real work, not demos

Frondose isn't another chatbot. It operates a real browser, executes multi-step workflows, and reports back with what it did.

Chrome-native

Controls your actual Chrome via the DevTools Protocol — the same browser, signed-in sessions, and extensions you already use. No sandboxed clone.

Conversational

Ask in plain English. "Find 50 UK manufacturers on LinkedIn" — Frondose plans the steps, shows its work, and checks in before anything goes out.

Approval gates

Outbound actions — connection requests, messages, posts — pause for your sign-off. In Auto mode you decide which gates to trust and skip.

Persistent memory

A local SQLite store with full-text search. Frondose remembers leads, conversations, and your preferences across every session.

Auto mode

Hand off a multi-step task and walk away. Frondose runs autonomously, streams its progress, and only pauses when it genuinely needs you.

Any website

LinkedIn is the sweet spot, but the browser primitives work on any HTTPS page — SaaS dashboards, research portals, verification flows.

Two modes, one agent

Supervise it, or set it loose

Switch between hands-on and autonomous with a single click. The agent, the memory, and the browser session stay the same.

Manual

Conversation-first. You guide each step. Frondose shows inline workflow cards, asks before anything goes out, and learns your preferences along the way.

  • Chat-driven task execution
  • Inline workflow cards
  • Approval before every outbound
  • Real-time page context

Auto

Execution-first. Frondose takes the wheel with a visible page-edge signal, a live action readout, and a collapsible chat dock you can reopen anytime.

  • Autonomous multi-step runs
  • Page-edge takeover signal
  • Live action timeline
  • Interrupt at any moment
How it works

From idea to execution in four steps

No scripts, no templates, no setup files. Just describe what you want.

1

Describe

"Find 50 UK plastics manufacturers on LinkedIn and add them to my outreach list."

2

Plan

Frondose breaks the goal into steps, shows you the workflow card, and asks for approval on anything outbound.

3

Execute

It drives your real Chrome — navigates, clicks, types, extracts — while you watch live or walk away.

4

Report

Results land in memory. Ask about those manufacturers next week and Frondose still remembers.
